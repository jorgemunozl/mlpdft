# ADR-0008: 40 Ry labels only, and an MD protocol that decorrelates

## Context

- **The provisional build mixed cutoffs:** bulk and F-doped at
  `ecutwfc = 30 Ry`, the interface at 40 Ry. The charter states 40/320 Ry.
- **30 Ry is not converged for stress.** The identical bulk LiF starting cell
  is at −406 kbar at 30 Ry and −35 kbar at 40 Ry (`LIF64_KJPAW_V2` and
  `_NPT_V3` against `LIF64_KJPAW_FINAL` and `_NPT_FINAL`).
- **The MD inputs set `ion_temperature = 'andersen'` without `nraise`.** QE's
  default `nraise = 1` re-thermalises every atom at every step, which makes the
  dynamics overdamped.
- **The repository's pseudopotential files are not byte-identical to the
  files the runs read.** QE printed MD5 sums Li `d2f1e9ef…` and F `48ea186a…`
  for the runs; the repository files are Li `4191b14a…` and F `f73bf51f…`.
  They are differently formatted copies of the same PSLibrary 1.0.0 files, for
  example in how quotes are escaped.
- **Both files suggest more than 40 Ry:** 50 Ry for Li and 49 Ry for F.

## Decision

- **Labels:** use only runs at `ecutwfc = 40`, `ecutrho = 320`. All 30 Ry and
  20 Ry runs are excluded from training and evaluation.
- **New calculations** copy the existing 40 Ry settings exactly:
  - PBE, PSLibrary 1.0.0 PAW;
  - 3×3×3 k-points, `nosym`, `conv_thr = 1e-8`;
  - the per-environment `mixing_beta`;
  - Methfessel–Paxton smearing 0.02 Ry for metals;
  - `dt = 10` a.u.;
  - QE 7.4.
- **Pseudopotentials:** new jobs use the repository files
  (`assets/quantum_expresso/pseudo`), and a checksum guard stops any job that
  reads other files.
- **Preflight before any MD:** a single point must reproduce the existing
  40 Ry bulk data at the same configuration: force RMSE ≤ 1 meV/Å, largest
  deviation ≤ 3 meV/Å, |ΔE| ≤ 1e-4 Ry. The stage 2 analysis repeats the check
  in every environment.
- **New MD:** `nraise = 100`. Run one SVR pilot and one Andersen pilot, and
  keep the thermostat that decorrelates forces faster. Every run starts from
  the ideal lattice with independent Gaussian displacements (σ = 0.06 Å).
- **Cutoff and k-point check:** single points at 30/40/60/80 Ry and 4×4×4.
  40 Ry is accepted if the force RMSE against 80 Ry is ≤ 10 meV/Å.

Job folders are generated by `src/mlpdft/utils/make_qe_campaign.py` into
`assets/quantum_expresso/campaign-2026-11/`.

## Consequences

- **Builds retired:** the provisional three-environment build and the interim
  builds, which used bulk and F-doped labels at 20–30 Ry.
- **Interface:** its data were already at 40 Ry, so only its role changes.
- **If the cutoff check fails** in some environment, the gap is reported as a
  label-noise floor. Recomputing the data is out of budget.
