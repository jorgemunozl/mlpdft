# ADR-000N: Short imperative title

## Context

What forced a decision. Include the constraint that made the default option
insufficient: budget, data, a measured failure, a deadline.

## Decision

One paragraph, present tense, specific enough that someone can implement it.
Name the numbers, the scope, and what is explicitly **not** included.

## Evidence

Measurements or runs that support the decision. Link to the run folder or the
method document; do not restate a whole report here.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what must be re-checked later.
State the cost of being wrong.
