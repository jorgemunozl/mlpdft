# Uncertainty Quantification for Foundation MACE Potentials in Lithium Diffusion

This study compares independent fine-tuning committees, warm-restart snapshot
ensembles, and multi-head fine-tuning as uncertainty-quantification methods for
a MACE foundation potential trained on lithium-containing DFT data. The methods
are evaluated by whether ensemble disagreement predicts held-out DFT force
errors and signals uncertainty in lithium diffusivity and effective diffusion
activation energy across bulk LiF, Li-rich Li-F, and LiF-interface environments.
